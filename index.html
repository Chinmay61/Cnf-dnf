01 · Learn

Definitions

Conjunctive Normal Form (CNF)

A formula in CNF is an AND (∧) of clauses, where each clause is an OR (∨) of literals.

(P ∨ Q) ∧ (¬P ∨ R)

Think: AND of ORs.

Disjunctive Normal Form (DNF)

A formula in DNF is an OR (∨) of terms, where each term is an AND (∧) of literals.

(P ∧ Q) ∨ (¬P ∧ R)

Think: OR of ANDs.

Literal: a proposition such as P or its negation ¬P. Clause: a disjunction of literals. Term: a conjunction of literals.

Important logical laws

LawEquivalent expression
De Morgan's law¬(P ∧ Q) ≡ ¬P ∨ ¬Q
De Morgan's law¬(P ∨ Q) ≡ ¬P ∧ ¬Q
Distribute OR over ANDP ∨ (Q ∧ R) ≡ (P ∨ Q) ∧ (P ∨ R)
Distribute AND over ORP ∧ (Q ∨ R) ≡ (P ∧ Q) ∨ (P ∧ R)
Double negation¬¬P ≡ P
02 · Worked examples

Step-by-step examples

Example 1: Convert to CNF

Given: P ∨ (Q ∧ R)

Apply the distributive law: distribute P ∨ across the conjunction.

P ∨ (Q ∧ R) ≡ (P ∨ Q) ∧ (P ∨ R)

Answer: (P ∨ Q) ∧ (P ∨ R), which is CNF (an AND of OR clauses).

Example 2: Convert to DNF

Given: P ∧ (Q ∨ R)

Apply the distributive law: distribute P ∧ across the disjunction.

P ∧ (Q ∨ R) ≡ (P ∧ Q) ∨ (P ∧ R)

Answer: (P ∧ Q) ∨ (P ∧ R), which is DNF (an OR of AND terms).

Summary

Remember

  • CNF: AND of OR clauses.
  • DNF: OR of AND terms.
  • Use distributive laws to convert between forms.
  • Use De Morgan's laws to push negations inward.
  • Equivalent formulas have the same truth value for every assignment.
